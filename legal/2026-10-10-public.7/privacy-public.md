## 1. Information we process

- Local app records: questions, board entries, marks, progress, preferences,
  cached access and prices, and pending reward, purchase or assistance deliveries.
  Account credentials and delivery records use protected device storage. Local
  play is not a general cloud backup of every board.
- Account information: a random guest identifier, support code, credential
  hashes, creation and activity times, sessions and recovery records, and an
  optional editable nickname. Optional Sign in with Apple supplies a verified
  identifier and, when provided, a name for the nickname. We request no Apple
  email address or password. A pseudonymous guest is not necessarily unidentifiable.
- Rewards, access and assistance: completion and reward references, balances
  and ledgers, access grants, recorded usage, pending deliveries and relevant
  purchase or ad verification. Completion checks receive solved-board evidence
  and retain completion metadata and proof hashes. Autofill requests and receipts
  include before-and-after board or path state and the proposed move; this does
  not send every subsequent edit or every saved board. These records verify
  delivery, prevent duplicate credits or charges and resolve incomplete operations.
- Apple transactions: product and transaction references, account association,
  purchase, renewal, expiry and revocation dates, quantities, delivery status and
  signed verification or refund evidence. Paid allowance claims record eligible
  covered dates. Apple handles card and store-account details; we do not receive
  your full card details or Apple password.
- Support and administration: messages, contact details, screenshots, logs and
  device/build information you provide, relevant private staff notes and reasoned
  corrective actions. Data-request delivery also uses the email address you provide,
  its verification status and the reviewed response/send receipt. Do not send
  passwords, usable credentials or unrelated
  sensitive information. A support code helps lookup but does not prove ownership.
- Connection and security information: IP address, request path, time, client
  details and response status. Limited rate-limit records help prevent abuse.
  Our application error logs use paths and error categories rather than request
  bodies, credentials or raw Apple evidence. Providers have their own security logs.
- Agreement records: Terms acceptance, Privacy acknowledgement, document
  versions and action times are stored on your device, not currently uploaded
  as an account agreement receipt. They are separate from advertising consent.

The website provides static information, legal documents and configuration. We
add no website analytics, ad SDK, sign-up form or agreement receipt. Cloudflare
receives ordinary request/security information and can use security cookies for
its protections. A visit does not create an account. Email links only send mail
when you choose to send it. We add no separate app analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

## 2. Advertising and your choices

Where enabled, Google provides ads and privacy messages. Its SDK can process IP
address and approximate location, device/app identifiers, software details,
ad views and interactions, performance and diagnostics. Non-personalised requests
can still involve identifiers and storage. We do not request precise location
or Apple's cross-app tracking permission.

Applicable consent or refusal choices precede permitted ad requests. Use Ad
privacy options to change them. Refusing optional consent does not stop free
puzzle play, although ad availability can change. Terms acceptance and Privacy
acknowledgement are not advertising consent.

Rewarded-ad requests send Google an account-bound identifier and scoped reward
reference, not your Apple name, email, nickname or puzzle answer. Completion
callbacks supply transaction and ad/reward details, which we verify and retain
with any resulting grant. Remove Ads suppresses automatic placements; optional
rewarded videos remain separate. Active Plus can provide eligible benefits
without their videos. These options do not erase a provider's earlier processing.

## 5. Getting your information

Export my data in Account provides an authenticated, machine-readable copy
of available server profile, wallet, completion, reward, access, assistance,
purchase/delivery and structured support-action records, including session-use
times. Usable credentials and raw provider evidence are excluded. Free-text staff
or security notes require human review for third-party details or protected
information. The copy identifies excluded categories and does not include all
local-only boards or records held by support, Apple or Google.

Use Request my data or contact privacy@versava.net for supplemental review.
Where email delivery is available, provide an email address for this request
and verify it with a short-lived code. This address is separate from your Apple
sign-in and is used for verification and the requested response, not marketing
or advertising. The address, verification status and send receipt are linked
to the request. Verification codes are not included in account exports.

An authorised administrator reviews the prepared account copy and other
requested records before choosing to send the response and data attachment
to your verified address. A mail provider accepting the send does not guarantee
arrival in your inbox. The reviewed reply remains available in the app. The
automatic copy and preparation status do not limit your statutory rights or
replace our response. Minimal received/prepared notifications to
privacy@versava.net contain no export or usable credentials.

Prepared copies are restricted, never public links, and available in our
services for at most 30 days from preparation. The approved attachment copy
uses the same expiry. Expiry or account deletion blocks further access and
sending. Service interruption can delay physical file cleanup; NAS copies
are excluded from routine backups and authorised recovery follows the same
expiry and erasures. Email copies already sent follow the recipient's mailbox
and communications providers' retention; deleting the app account cannot
recall an email. Request email, review and send records remain with the account
until final deletion, subject to the retention information below.

Saving or sharing your export is your choice. The app removes its temporary
sharing file afterwards; copies you save or send follow your chosen destination.

## 6. Deletion and retention

Delete account under Contact support opens an in-app confirmation. This
starts a 14-calendar-day grace period and shows the exact deletion date. The
account remains usable; Stop deletion cancels the request until finalisation
begins. Signing out or uninstalling does not cancel it. At finalisation we remove
live account records, including staff account notes, history, balances, access,
unclaimed banked allowances and purchase associations added during the grace
period, and end sessions and recovery access. The app clears related local
records after it confirms that final deletion has finished; other devices'
local copies are not remotely erased.

Deletion does not cancel Apple billing, erase Apple or Google records, or revive
old history on a new account. Valid Remove Ads or active Plus can be verified
through explicit Restore Purchases on a new account without restoring spent
consumables or deleted records. New Plus eligibility begins on the next UTC date,
without backfill. We do not retain fresh Apple authorisation through the grace
period; the app explains any Apple-settings disconnection needed afterwards.

Limited deletion-status/cancellation credential hashes and non-personal outcomes
remain for up to 31 days, along with a restricted account-identifier/deletion-time
marker to apply erasures to restored service. They do not retain the deleted
profile, wallet or purchase history. Restricted database recovery copies can
retain earlier information for up to 30 days; prior deletions are reapplied
before restored service resumes. Deletion is not instant erasure of every
provider snapshot.

Active account and delivery records are retained for requested services,
reconciliation, security and rights. Expired credentials and temporary security
records are cleaned regularly. Support correspondence, delivered notifications,
provider logs and necessary legal-claim records are kept only for their relevant
purpose and reviewed accordingly. A specific duty or unresolved claim can justify
limited retention, not indefinite retention of every account field. Local records
remain subject to device storage and your actions.

Hong Kong law provides access and correction rights. Where applicable, you also
have rights to erasure, restriction, portability, objection to legitimate-interest
processing, consent withdrawal and complaint to the relevant regulator. Use
Account or contact privacy@versava.net. We may reasonably verify ownership
without requesting usable credentials by email. Where GDPR applies, we normally
respond within one month; any lawful extension is explained within that month.
We explain justified restrictions or redactions. Self-service exports and deletion
confirmation do not waive your rights.

Encrypted connections, protected device storage, credential hashing and restricted
access reduce unauthorised access; no system is absolutely secure. Protect your
device and report suspected compromise. Revisions identify edition and date, with
material-change notice and separate consent where required.
