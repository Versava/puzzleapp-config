## 3. Accounts, progress, stars, and diamonds

When the app first connects to our account service, it can create a guest
account with a random identifier and a private credential. Knowing the guest
identifier or non-secret support code alone does not provide access to its wallet.
A support code helps staff find the correct account when you request help; it
does not prove account ownership or recover lost guest credentials. You may optionally link
the guest account to Sign in with Apple. A link to an Apple identity that already
belongs to another Daily Pause account does not automatically merge balances.
If you explicitly switch to an existing Apple account, its wallet and history
remain separate from the guest account. Any saved guest credential remains
subject to its validity and device-storage limits. An optional player nickname
can help identify your account; it must not impersonate someone else or contain
unrelated sensitive information.

Protect your device and credentials. Do not share, sell, rent, or transfer an
account, impersonate another tester, or claim another person's rewards. Your
own account may be used on supported devices, but local puzzle saves do not
currently form a general cross-device backup service.

Progress saved only on a device may be lost after clearing app data, removal,
or a test reset. If you lose an unlinked guest credential, we may be unable to
recover that account. Signing out, removing the app, or leaving TestFlight does
not itself delete server-held records. The Privacy Notice explains requests
concerning those records.

Stars and diamonds are virtual access credits with no cash value. They cannot
be withdrawn as money or transferred between accounts. The server-confirmed
wallet determines the balance available for a supported unlock or exchange.
A locally completed puzzle or pending offline claim does not by itself create
a spendable credit; eligibility and duplicate claims are checked when the claim
reaches the service.

The current Beta awards one star for the first eligible Today completion and
two further stars for finishing its daily set. Past completions do not earn
daily stars. Where a past day is offered for star redemption, one star unlocks
the whole selected day, subject to confirmation by the service. The app shows
the applicable cost before a supported redemption. These are testing rules;
future offers and rule changes will be disclosed before they apply.

The current diamond rule awards one diamond for each group of 14 consecutive
fully completed puzzle dates, with further milestones at 28, 42, and so on.
This follows the dates of the puzzles, not consecutive app visits or play
sessions. A date qualifies only after the service verifies all four puzzles
from the same supported daily release. Each date counts once; replaying it or
using another release does not count it again. Verified Today and authorised
Past completions can qualify, and completing a missing eligible Past date can
join a chain. Practice puzzles and pack levels do not qualify. Past completion
still does not award daily stars.

Only puzzle dates on or after the UTC date when your account was first created
by our service are eligible. An existing account keeps its recorded original
creation date. Earlier puzzle dates and future dates cannot qualify, even if
you have access to their content. Offline play before account registration
does not move this cutoff backwards. Apple linking does not reset that account's
cutoff; switching to another account uses that account's separate records.
Access to a Past date and a supported question are required when its proof is
verified. Opening or unlocking a date alone does not complete it.

You may exchange one confirmed diamond for 50 stars through the current online
offer. No reverse star-to-diamond exchange is offered. An exchange requires
server confirmation and cannot spend the same diamond twice. Spending or
exchanging diamonds does not reduce the lifetime milestone-award record or
allow those milestones to be earned again. Currency obtained through a purchase,
if purchase testing is later enabled, is separate from that milestone record.

The current First Steps collection has one released pack for each of eight
games. Each pack contains 120 fixed levels: 60 Easy followed by 60 Medium, with
a 20-star unlock shown before confirmation. The unlock concerns that released
pack and its questions; a later separately released pack is not automatically
included. Existing grants and saved questions for earlier prototype packs remain
separate historical records. Direct diamond pack prices are not configured,
and paid pack checkout is disabled.

Each puzzle question allows at most three hints for the account using it. The
first is free, the second costs one star, and the third costs two stars. The
app shows the cost before confirmation. A hint highlights a cell to consider
or a conflicting entry; it does not fill in an answer. Restarting or undoing
a board does not replenish its hint allowance. The free first hint can work
offline and is recorded on the device, including before a guest account is
registered. Paid hints need an online account and a server-confirmed grant.
Retries of the same confirmed grant do not create another charge or allowance.

Where a verified rewarded video is offered, you may choose it instead of the
star cost for the second or third hint. All methods share the same three-hint
limit. An ad-funded hint requires our service to verify the ad provider's
signed completion notice; a local video-completion message alone does not
grant it. Publisher ads and these live ad-funded grants remain disabled in
the current Beta. Sample videos do not grant paid hints.
