## 3. Rewards, currencies, packs and hints

Stars and diamonds are virtual access credits. They have no cash withdrawal or
redemption value and cannot be transferred between accounts. Their confirmed
server balances govern spending. A completed offline board or pending claim
does not alone create spendable currency; the service checks eligibility,
proofs, account ownership and duplicate claims when received.

A supported newcomer offer gives an eligible newly created Daily Pause account
30 stars and one diamond in the current smaller diamond unit. It is available
once for that account and needs an online Collect action and service confirmation.
Account creation or opening the popup alone does not credit the wallet. Retrying
a confirmed collection, signing in again or linking the same account to Apple
does not give another gift. Accounts created before this offer's eligibility
starts do not receive it retroactively. It is separate from daily claims,
completed-puzzle rewards and purchases, has no cash value and cannot be moved to
another account. The gift record and any remaining balance are erased with the
account; they are not restored as an old balance on a replacement account.

The current free daily selection contains four puzzles. An eligible Today
completion earns one star for the first puzzle and two further stars for the
full set. A separate manual Today claim gives one free star per account and
server UTC date; an unclaimed free allowance expires at that date's end.
An eligible rewarded-video or active Plus daily bonus gives one further star,
sharing one limit per current UTC date. Past completions do not earn these
Today stars. A supported Past-day unlock covers that whole date's selection;
the app shows its current one-star cost or eligible alternative before use.

Seven consecutive fully completed eligible puzzle dates award ten diamonds,
with further milestones at 14, 21 and so on. This follows the puzzle dates,
not consecutive visits. The service verifies all four questions from the same
supported daily release. A date counts once, and previously rewarded dates are
not rewarded again. Authorised Past completion can join a chain; packs and
practice do not count. Only dates on or after the account's original server
creation UTC date are eligible. Future dates cannot qualify early.

Each reviewed pack contains one puzzle game and its identified fixed questions.
The current collection has 96 packs across eight games, each with 120 levels.
First Steps, Step 2 and Step 3 contain Easy–Medium levels; Easy, Medium and Hard
collections use their named difficulty. Separate future packs are not included
automatically. Confirmed permanent access concerns the identified pack, subject
to service availability and supported devices; it is not a promise of an
indefinitely operated service or unlimited future releases.

Each pack has one required price: stars, diamonds, or stars plus diamonds. A
combined price requires both amounts and does not offer a currency choice.
The app shows the current quote before confirmation. The service checks its
revision and charges both amounts together; an insufficient balance or changed
offer grants nothing and charges neither amount. Existing ownership is not
charged again. New diamond-to-star or star-to-diamond exchanges are unavailable.

The first hint for a question is free, the second costs one star, the third
costs two stars, and each further hint costs one diamond. It highlights a useful
or conflicting cell rather than filling an answer. Undo or restart does not
reset recorded hint usage. Where offered, a verified video or active Plus can
cover hints two and three; every method shares those hint numbers. Neither
covers the diamond cost from the fourth hint onwards. Paid hints need online
confirmation; retrying an already confirmed grant does not charge twice.
