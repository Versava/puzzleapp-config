## 1. Information we process

We obtain personal data from you, your device, your use of the service and
relevant providers. Depending on the features you use, we process the following
categories. Examples within them include, but are not limited to:

- Account information: account and guest identifiers, nickname, sign-in and
  access information.
- Gameplay and activity: progress, scores, preferences, rewards, balances and
  content access.
- Purchase and advertising information: transactions, entitlements, ad
  interactions and performance, and related estimates.
- Technical and security information: IP address, approximate location, device
  and app information, connection details and misuse or fault records.
- Communications and administration: contact details, messages, supplied
  material and relevant support or administrative records.
- Agreement and privacy records: applicable documents, acceptance,
  acknowledgements and choices.

Without information necessary for a requested feature, we cannot provide that
feature. You do not need to provide unrelated optional information to play free
puzzles. Payment providers handle payment information; we do not receive full card
details or your provider password.

The website processes ordinary hosting and security information, including
security cookies where used. It has no advertising or optional analytics, and
visiting it does not create an account.

## 2. Advertising and personalisation

We use advertising information to measure and optimise advertising performance
and verify rewards. Where enabled, advertising providers also process technical
and advertising information for ad delivery, measurement, optimisation,
diagnostics and fraud prevention. Non-personalised ads may still use identifiers
or storage.

We apply your advertising privacy choices before requesting ads. You can
change applicable choices through Ad privacy options. Refusing optional consent does
not prevent free puzzle play, but may affect advertising availability.
Accepting the Terms or acknowledging this notice does not provide that consent.

We use service activity and technical information to analyse, improve and
personalise the service, including gameplay and benefits. This can include
automated analysis. This optional processing relies on your consent, separate
from advertising consent. You can give or withdraw consent in Account → Privacy
choices. Withdrawing consent stops this optional processing in the app and
initiates removal of the related activity and personal analysis records. An
offline app request remains pending until successfully sent to our service.
After withdrawal of personalisation consent, existing puzzles and progress may
remain as service records. We do not supply this activity history for
third-party targeted advertising.

## 3. Purposes and lawful grounds

We process information to provide requested play, accounts, purchases and
benefits; verify transactions; administer and support the service; prevent
misuse and investigate faults; handle requests and claims; and meet legal
obligations. Advertising and personalisation follow section 2.

Where applicable, we rely on contract performance for requested services;
legitimate interests for proportionate administration, security, fraud
prevention, fault investigation, support and protection of legal interests;
legal obligations for required processing; and separate consent for processing
that requires it. Withdrawal does not affect earlier lawful processing. Under
applicable Hong Kong rules, an unrelated new use requires prescribed consent
unless a lawful exception applies.

## 4. Recipients and international processing

Authorised personnel and providers receive information for the purposes above.
Provider functions include hosting, storage, security, supported sign-in and
payments, advertising, communications and request handling. Providers include,
but are not limited to, Cloudflare for hosting, storage and security, Apple for
supported account and store functions, and Google for advertising. Necessary
disclosures to authorities or professional advisers may be made for legal
duties, service protection or claims, subject to applicable law. We do not sell
account or puzzle histories.

We are based in Hong Kong. Processing may occur there, in the EEA and elsewhere
our providers or authorised personnel operate. International processing is
subject to applicable transfer requirements and safeguards. Contact
privacy@versava.net for relevant arrangements and available copies.

## 5. Your rights and data requests

Applicable rights include access and correction and, where provided by law,
erasure, restriction, portability, objection and consent withdrawal. Contact
privacy@versava.net or use available Account functions. We may reasonably verify
identity and authority and make lawful redactions to protect others, security
or other protected information. Where GDPR applies, we respond within one month
or notify you within that period of a lawful extension. You may complain to the
relevant data-protection regulator.

Use Account to export supported account and activity records in a
machine-readable format. Standard exports do not limit valid statutory requests.
Contact details supplied for a request are used to handle it, not for marketing
or advertising.

We may email a private download link. Prepared request copies are accessible for
at most 30 days from preparation, ending earlier on final account deletion or a
change to the recorded recipient or copy. They are excluded from routine
backups. Service interruptions may delay physical removal after access expires.
Keep links private; expiry or deletion cannot recall emails or copies already
saved or shared elsewhere.

## 6. Deletion and retention

Requests for account deletion are processed according to the schedule shown in
the app. During any available grace period, the account remains usable and the
request can be cancelled until finalisation begins. Final deletion ends account
access and removes associated live records, subject to limited retention below.
It does not cancel separately billed subscriptions or delete records that
providers retain independently under their own privacy policies and legal
obligations. Nor can it recall copies saved elsewhere. See
[account help](https://puzzle.versava.net/support/) for cancellation and purchase
recovery arrangements.

### On your device

App activity histories use a rolling 30-day period; personalisation examples,
such as past interactions and outcomes, use a rolling 90-day period. Expired
records on inactive devices are removed when the app next accesses them.

### On our service

The active service retains activity summaries for up to 30 days after receipt;
personalisation examples for up to 90 days from the interaction, and received
evaluation records for up to 90 days after receipt.

Limited records needed to complete deletion and preserve its effect remain for
up to 31 days after final account deletion. Our recovery policy limits restricted
copies to 30 days from creation and requires later erasures to be reapplied
before restoration.

We retain shared analysis results only while needed for service operation or
evaluation.

Other records, including authorised analysis copies and correspondence, are
retained only for the relevant service, evaluation, support, security or legal
purpose. The retention period depends on completion of that purpose, applicable
legal requirements and unresolved claims. Requests concerning personal data in
shared analysis results or other retained records are handled under section 5.

## 7. Security and updates

We use safeguards including restricted access and encrypted connections, but
cannot guarantee absolute security. We update the date above when this notice
changes and provide material-change notice and separate consent where required.
